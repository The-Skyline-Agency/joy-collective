Meet the founder

The story behind the joy

Adriana Cox — Founder

In the hardest season of her life, Adriana Cox went through a deep depression. What helped wasn't one big breakthrough — it was small, quiet moments of joy. Noticing them became a path out.

Joy Collective was born from that path: a place for women to walk it together, in small groups, with real conversations about the hard stuff.
